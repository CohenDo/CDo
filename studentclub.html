Our Mission

The Student Technology Club helps students develop technology skills, explore computer science, and work together on creative projects. Our goal is to build a welcoming community where students can learn and grow.

Benefits of Membership

  • Learn new computer and technology skills.
  • Collaborate with other students.
  • Build projects for your portfolio.
  • Improve problem-solving and teamwork.
  • Explore future technology careers.

Upcoming Activities

Our planned activities include:

  1. Introduction to HTML and CSS workshop.
  2. Group website design challenge.
  3. Beginner programming practice session.
  4. Student technology project showcase.

These are proposed activities for the club.

Technology Resources

Members can use these websites to learn and practice:

  • MDN HTML Learning Resources
  • W3Schools CSS Tutorials
  • GitHub Project Hosting

How to Participate

Students interested in joining can follow these steps:

  1. Learn about the club's goals and activities.
  2. Prepare a technology topic you want to explore.
  3. Contact the club advisor or student organizer.
